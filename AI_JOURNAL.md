## How I worked
- **Planning and tool setup before the timer (not timed):** a separate Claude session with only the brief. The timer started at Step 1 (21:55). The result is `PLAN.md`, my own working plan, kept outside the repo.
- **One session per step, the same prompt each time:** only this step; no libraries, layers or features that aren't in the plan; update the docs; verify with build and tests; draft *Asked* and *AI gave*, but leave *Decision* and *Why* to me; don't commit.
- **Two reviewers at the risky steps (2, 4, 5):** Claude and Codex both reviewed `git diff HEAD~1`, and I decided what to fix.
- Several *Decision* / *Why* cells were worded by the AI from my choices (I asked it to).

4. **Step 2:** I swapped the AI's New Zealand for Indonesia, based on where the insurer actually operates in APAC.
5. **Step 2:** The AI left settling above the reserve as an open question. I asked for a worked example, then chose option B (you can't settle above the reserve).
6. **Step 2 and Step 5 reviews:** I didn't fix every finding by default. I asked which ones actually mattered, then fixed those.
7. **Step 3:** The journal times the AI wrote were wrong, so I had them corrected.
8. **Step 4:** I added Codex as a second reviewer. I applied Claude's three security fixes and left out Codex's Scalar "Authorize" button.
9. **Redesign:** My idea. v1 worked but looked unfinished.
10. **Timeline bug:** I spotted in the browser that the claim timeline was cut off at the bottom and had it fixed (`339d9e1`).
